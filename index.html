02 / Experience

Where I've worked

2025 - Present

Computer Scientist

U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Engineering Research and Development Center, Construction Engineering Research Laboratory · Champaign, IL

Tested, documented, and demonstrated Control system Agnostic Secure Enclave (CASE) across 8 buildings at 2 sites, validating secure communication, installation, and scalability while achieving cost savings exceeding 90% compared with vendor alternatives.

Awarded $197K to fund the FY27 Control System Industry Forum, bringing together 200+ industry and tri-service partners from 90+ companies to drive immediate and future updates to critical UFC, UFGS, and UFS criteria and reinforce the team's role as a government criteria leader.

Contributed to UFGS specification development by defining construction scopes of work, technical requirements, and regulatory standards for building-control projects.

Defined standardized test plans for building control systems to evaluate functionality, interoperability, maintainability, and cybersecurity, establishing consistent testing procedures across laboratory capabilities.

Presented laboratory and project capabilities to government, academic, and industry stakeholders through conferences and technical tours, communicating project impact to support funding, collaboration, and technology adoption.

  • Cybersecurity, Networking, Oversight & Governance
  • Building Automation Systems (BAS)
2024 - 2025

Cyber Resilience Engineering Intern

U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Engineering Research and Development Center, Construction Engineering Research Laboratory · Champaign, IL

Tested and documented the implementation of the Control system Agnostic Secure Enclave (CASE), a drop-in cybersecurity solution designed to secure legacy systems, ensuring secure communication and smooth integration while preparing for future scalability to meet evolving cybersecurity standards. Documentation included in the CASE Field Guide and Final Report. Project details can be found here: https://serdp-estcp.mil/projects/details/f030eb66-f630-4008-a92f-378b5326007f/ew18-5355-project-overview

Developed and automated wireless communication methods for Robotics Operating System 2 (ROS2), enhancing device-to-device communication while resolving compatibility issues and ensuring that communication adhered to best security and safety practices.

Constructed engaging data visualizations in Shiny for Python and Dash to facilitate migration from Tableau to open-source alternatives for the Corps Reduced and Abridged FEMP Tool (CRAFT), increasing user satisfaction, generating thousands in savings from licensing fees.

  • Cybersecurity, Networking, Data Visualization
  • Python, Robotics
